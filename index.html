Why this exists

You shouldn't have to discover a pothole by hitting one.

Driving an unfamiliar road in India means playing a guessing game. The data to map road damage exists — it's just trapped in PDFs and dashcam footage nobody processes. Pothole Patrol takes that footage, runs it through a YOLOv8 model trained on 10,000+ annotated road images, and turns raw detections into a colour-coded map anyone can read in seconds. The same intuition as Google Maps traffic layers — applied to the road surface itself.

YOLOv8m detection
Optimised for 30fps dashcam inference. Detects potholes, longitudinal cracks, alligator cracks and more.
GPS-mapped output
Every detection plotted on an interactive Folium map with severity colour-coding — built for real decisions.
Unified dataset
Custom pipeline resolved label conflicts across RDD2022 and Pothole.v1 — 10GB+ of merged, clean training data.
